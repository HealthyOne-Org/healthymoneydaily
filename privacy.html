Privacy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Healthy Money Daily (healthymoneydaily.com) is run by Hardaway Labs LLC. This page covers the website only. The Budget Shield app has its own privacy policy.

What we collect

  • Page views and clicks. We use PostHog to count which pages are read and which App Store links are tapped. PostHog records things like the page address, the referring site, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. We don't use it to build a profile of you by name.
  • Hosting logs. Our host, Vercel, keeps standard server logs (IP address, time, page requested) to run and secure the site.
  • Email. If you write to us, we keep your message so we can reply.

We don't run advertising networks on this site, we don't sell personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Cookies and storage

PostHog stores a random identifier in your browser so repeat visits count once. The site also remembers, in your browser only, if you closed the Budget Shield bar at the bottom of a post. You can clear both in your browser settings, and blocking them doesn't break the site.

Links to other sites

We link to official sources such as the Federal Reserve and the Bureau of Labor Statistics, and to the App Store. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Children

This site is for adults and isn't directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at support@healthyonefinances.com. Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have additional rights, and we'll honor them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
