How we write about money

  • Official numbers first

    Prices come from the Bureau of Labor Statistics, rates from the Federal Reserve, rules from the CFPB and the Federal Register. We link every one.

  • Education, not advice

    We explain how money works. We don't tell you what to invest in, and we never recommend a stock.

  • Odds are not forecasts

    When we mention market odds for a Fed move, we say what they are: what traders are betting, not what will happen.
